Quest location photographs and guide captions

Original guide pages and captions:
Escape from Tarkov Wiki / Fandom
https://escapefromtarkov.fandom.com/
Each quest photo card links to its original quest page. Guide content is read
through the public MediaWiki parse API. Only the Guide section is used.
The fallback metadata for Delivery From the Past and Missing Informant was
checked on 2026-11-13. Fifteen captions were also translated into Korean.
